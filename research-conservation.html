Biodiversity change & conservation

Highlighted past, present and future research

Staude et al. 2025 · New Phytologist

Many non-native plant species are threatened in parts of their native range

Can a species be successful abroad while disappearing from its native range? Among more than 9,000 naturalized plant species, over 25% are threatened in at least part of their native range — showing that non-native success and conservation concern can coexist.

Garbowski et al.2026 · Coming soon

Unifying functional and phylogenetic dimensions of biodiversity for improved restoration science and practice

Can restoration recover not only species numbers, but also the functional strategies and evolutionary history needed for resilient ecosystems? Our simulations show that even relatively small restoration mixes can recover substantial functional and phylogenetic diversity — but only when species are selected from pools with sufficient trait and lineage breadth.

In preparation

Unifying regional Red Lists into a global plant threat database

How can we compare plant threat across regions when Red Lists are published in different languages and use different threat categories? We are compiling regional plant Red Lists from around the world and harmonizing their assessments towards a common IUCN-based framework, creating a global database of regional plant threat and local extinction.
