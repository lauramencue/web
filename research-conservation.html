Biodiversity change & conservation

Understanding biodiversity loss and improving the data and tools we use to conserve it.

A species rarely goes extinct everywhere at once. It thins out somewhere — usually at an edge of its range — long before it is lost. In the PaDRE project I ask how predictable those edges are, by assembling national and regional Red Lists of vascular plants into one comparable dataset and matching it against ecological niches, traits, phylogenies and spatial data. The goal is to separate how much of a regional extinction is explained by a species being ecologically marginal there, how much by what kind of plant it is, and how much by its evolutionary history.

As part of this work I am also helping build VascularPlantsNRL, an open database bringing together national and regional Red Lists of vascular plants from across Europe — the kind of shared, comparable resource this sort of analysis depends on, and one that other researchers can build on too.
