Genetic diversity & population change

How populations change through space and time, from demographic history to contemporary decline.

Much of this work is computational: population structure, admixture, bottlenecks, demographic models and phylogenetic inference, built from genome-wide data — often double-digest RAD sequencing (ddRADseq) — collected from species across their ranges. The aim is to read, in a genome, the history of a population: when it contracted, when it expanded, and how connected it still is to its neighbours.

I work mainly in R and on the Unix command line, on high-performance computing clusters, and I care a lot about turning results into figures that actually explain something.

Tools

Structure, Bottleneck, BEAST, MrBayes, RAxML, Admixture, Plink, Stacks, BayesAss.
